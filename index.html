OUR HERITAGE

Crafting Extraordinary Events. Creating Lasting Memories.

Established in 1988 by brothers G. Poornachandra Rao and G. Srinivasa Rao, Hubli Shamiyana Suppliers started with humble equipment rentals and has grown into North Karnataka's most trusted name in monumental event architecture.

Today, supported by an experienced workforce of over 180 personnel—including senior site managers, master welders, electricians, drapers, and transportation fleets—we deliver monumental stages, German aluminium hangars, luxury wedding mandaps, and government protocol setups across Hubballi, Dharwad, Hospet, Belagavi, and across the state.
